# ReGeneva YouTube video conventions

Applies to every ReGeneva YouTube video edit.

## Fixed brand clips (the same for every video)
| File | Length | Where it goes | Contents |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ReGeneva_intro.mp4` | 6 s | Very start of every video | ReGeneva logo with a short audio sting |
| `ReGeneva_outro.mp4` | 6 s | Very end of every video | Logo, like/share/subscribe/bell, QR code to book a free consult, plus a voice line |

- Use them unchanged. If one is updated, add a version suffix (`ReGeneva_intro_v2.mp4`) and use the newest version.
- Never show the QR code anywhere else in a video. It lives in the outro only.

## Per-video files
`<TOPIC>_<NN>_<short-name>.<ext>`
- On-camera clips: `CBAM_01_cold-open.MOV`, `CBAM_02_importer-pays.MOV`, and so on, numbered in script order.
- Empty-room reference clips: `<TOPIC>_room_no-chair.MOV` and `<TOPIC>_room_with-chair.MOV`.
- Screen captures: `<TOPIC>_sc_ch<N>_<what>.mp4`.
- Send original camera files through a Google Drive folder set to "Anyone with the link: Viewer". Never send WhatsApp-compressed copies.

## Editing standards
- Keep the presenter's chair. Replace only the room behind it with the virtual studio.
- Keep the area behind the presenter's head light enough that black hair stays clearly separated.
- Use the empty-room clips to remove the old wall colour exactly from hair and hand edges, with no white, grey or blurred patches. Before delivery, check zoomed-in frames at every fast hand or head movement.
- Narration: Surya Indian Accent Original Voice Clone, unless asked otherwise.
- Add two subtle subscribe/bell reminders mid-video at natural pauses, about 3 s each.
- Never speed up narration or on-camera speech. Trim pauses only.
