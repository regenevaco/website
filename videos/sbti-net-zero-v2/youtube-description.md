## Description

On 11 June 2026, the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) published Version 2.0 of its Corporate Net-Zero Standard, the biggest rewrite of the net-zero rulebook since 2021. In this explainer, ReGeneva covers what V2.0 is, what changed from V1, and what companies setting SBTi targets need to do now.

You'll learn:
✅ The V2.0 timeline: published June 2026, validations open February 2027, mandatory for new submissions from 1 February 2028
✅ Category A vs Category B companies, and the exact thresholds
✅ The new target architecture, and why net-zero targets are now optional
✅ Separate Scope 1 and Scope 2 targets, location-based Scope 2 and the new low-carbon electricity rules
✅ The Scope 3 "5% significance test" that replaces the 67% / 90% coverage thresholds
✅ The implementation hierarchy, and why carbon credits don't count toward targets
✅ Ongoing Emissions Responsibility (OER) and the 2035 carbon removal requirement
✅ Assurance, transition plans and annual reporting
✅ A 6-step checklist for getting ready
